#### Lisbon, Portugal
Perched on the Atlantic coast, Lisbon is a historic city defined by its relationship with the sea. Its iconic Tagus estuary has shaped the city's maritime heritage for centuries.

#### Barcelona, Spain
Nestled between the mountains and the Mediterranean, Barcelona boasts a unique urban layout that opens directly onto the sea. The port area serves as a bustling hub for international trade and leisure.

#### Marseille, France
As France's oldest city, Marseille offers a rugged Mediterranean coastline. Its deep-water port has been a gateway to the Mediterranean since antiquity.

#### Venice, Italy
Built on a series of small islands in a shallow lagoon, Venice is one of the world's most unique coastal cities. Its structure faces constant challenges from rising sea levels.

#### Copenhagen, Denmark
With its harbour at the heart of the city, Copenhagen is an example of sustainable urban planning and water quality management in a Northern European coastal setting.
