#### Hanoi (2015)
The starting point of the study: Hanoi's urban core stands out against surrounding farmland and water bodies.
This 2015 view marks the beginning of a ten-year PALSAR-2 record over Hanoi, rendered here in the HH (co-polarized) channel of JAXA's annual yearly mosaic. This all-weather L-band record is what allows the study to track urban expansion continuously, without the seasonal cloud gaps that limit the optical datasets used elsewhere in this analysis.
