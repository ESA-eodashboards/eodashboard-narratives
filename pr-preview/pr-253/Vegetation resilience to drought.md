## Challenge
Droughts are slow-onset disasters that unfold live-threatening impacts on ecosystems and people's livelihoods. Such impacts range from crop loss and increase in food insecurity, but also result in the reduction of drinking water, displacement or power shortages. Given that drought conditions can persist over several months, the challenge lies in understanding not only in describing the negative impacts but also studying the recovery time of different landscape systems.

## Objective
The objective of this study was to compare the recovery trajectory of different land cover types and ecosystems after severe drought impacts in order to understand how this recovery unfolds and differs based on the present ecosystem.

#### Southern African Drought in 2023/24
The rainy season October 2023 to March 2024 was characterized by late season onset and values 50% below the usual rainfall amount. This caused widespread impact across the whole region, resulting in the declaration of emergency state in Malawi and Zambia.

Widespread humanitarian assistance was requested to alleviate the acute food insecurity across the continent.
