#### Harvesting Stage (26 Sep – 31 Oct 2025)
The final composite of the season, corresponding to harvest. The study's own results found that Sentinel-1 VV backscatter combined with dry biomass during this stage was the strongest single predictor of rice yield in Suphan Buri — making this ALOS-2 view a useful comparison point for how L-band responds at the same point in the crop cycle.
