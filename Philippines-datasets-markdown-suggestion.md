#### Establishing a Baseline (2019, HH)
Davao de Oro (formerly Compostela Valley), Mindanao, is largely covered by tropical forest and small farms, an area under increasing pressure from illegal settlement and encroachment. This 2019 composite, in ALOS-2 PALSAR-2's HH channel, sets the starting point of the record: a relatively undisturbed forest landscape before any known clearing.

#### Baseline Canopy Structure (2019, HV)
The same 2019 date, now in the HV channel. Since HV is more sensitive to canopy volume than HH, this view is the reference the study's anomaly-detection approach depends on: any future weakening of the HV signal in a given area, relative to this baseline, is a candidate sign of forest disturbance beneath the canopy, potentially before it becomes visible in optical imagery.

#### One Year On (2020, HH)
Moving forward one year. Backscatter over most of the province remains broadly similar to 2019, which is itself informative: a stable baseline is what makes any localized deviation, at any given spot, stand out as worth investigating in the years that follow.

#### Five Years Later (2024, HH)
The most recent composite in this series, five years on from the baseline. This is where a viewer can look for visible structural change against the 2019 starting point — any clearing or degradation that occurred over the intervening years should now show up as a shift in backscatter over the affected areas.
